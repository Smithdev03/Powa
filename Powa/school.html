Our Inspiring Journey

From Humble Beginnings to Abundant Growth

PALACE OF WISDOM ACADEMY was established in the year 2022 in the historic city of Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria, starting humbly with 23 pupils and 2 dedicated teachers.

Through divine grace, unrelenting commitment to standards, and the overwhelming trust of our esteemed parents, we have increased abundantly! Today, we operate across two modern, well-equipped campuses with hundreds of thriving learners, a robust faculty, and an unmatched record of child transformation.

Our Mission

At Palace of Wisdom Academy, we are committed to providing holistic, quality education that develops the intellectual, moral, and creative potential of every child through dedicated teaching, modern learning methods, and a strong foundation of wisdom, discipline, and excellence.

Our Vision

To be a Palace where wisdom, knowledge, and character are built — raising godly, confident, and future-ready leaders who will transform their world.

We offer a complete, seamless educational ladder consisting of: Creche & Daycare (from 3 months), Nursery School (Nursery 1 & 2), Primary School (Grades 1 through 6), and College comprising Junior Secondary (JSS 1 – 3) and Senior Secondary (SSS 1 – 3) with Science, Arts, and Commercial departments.

Applying is fast and stress-free! You can click the Apply Now (WhatsApp) button to start the process immediately, or visit either of our two campuses in Abeokuta to obtain an admission form from our administrative desk.

Requirements include: a completed application form, photocopy of the child's birth certificate, 4 passport photographs, previous academic term reports (for transfer students), and participation in a brief, friendly placement evaluation.

We operate two accessible campuses in Abeokuta city, Ogun State:
Campus 1: No. 11 Ibon Street, Ijemo Agbadu, Abeokuta.
Campus 2: No. 5, Kowope Junction Oke Aregbe, Abeokuta.

Yes! We operate dedicated, comfortable, and well-maintained school buses covering major areas and neighborhoods in Abeokuta. Each bus is manned by an experienced driver and an attentive bus minder for complete pupil safety.
